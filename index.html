About us

At P2P Career, we believe that every young person deserves the opportunity to discover their purpose, grow with confidence, and pursue a meaningful career.

"P2P" stands for Potential to Profession, reflecting our belief that everyone has unique strengths that can lead to a fulfilling and impactful career when properly guided.

Our work is guided by five core values that shape our approach and reflect our commitment to youth empowerment and inclusive development.

Whether you're a student, a recent graduate or early in your working life, we meet you where you are and help you take the next step with clarity.

What guides us

Five core values

These values shape how we coach, how we design our programmes, and how we show up for every young person we work with.

1Value one

Empowering Potential

We believe that every individual has untapped strengths. With the right support and opportunities, anyone can transform their potential into a meaningful profession.

2Value two

Purpose-Driven Action

We encourage youth to move forward with clarity and intention, making career choices aligned with their values, interests, and long-term goals.

3Value three

Confidence through Support

We foster a safe and encouraging space where young people are supported to believe in themselves, overcome doubts, and take the next step with courage.

4Value four

Lifelong Growth

We promote continuous learning, adaptability, and resilience, helping youth develop the mindset to grow through every stage of life and career.

5Value five

Inclusive Access

We are committed to providing fair and open access to career guidance and development opportunities, regardless of background, education, or resources.

Questions

Frequently asked

Can't find your answer? Get in touch and we'll be happy to help.

Who can join P2P Career programmes?

Our programmes are designed for young people aged 18–30, including students, recent graduates and early-career professionals, from any background.

How much do the sessions cost?

Our sessions are based on what each participant needs from coaching. We keep fees affordable because our vision is to pay it forward, and we connect you with the right professionals for your needs.

Are sessions online or in person?

Sessions are remote, so you can join from anywhere. We arrange each 1:1 session at a time that suits you and your coach.

How does 1:1 coaching work?

Our coaching is personal and one-to-one. After you register, we learn about your needs and match you with a coach or professional who fits your goals. Together you work through your strengths, career direction and next steps across your coaching sessions.

How do I register?
